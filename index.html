Haoyu Wang

PhD student in Financial Engineering
Department of Accounting and Finance, University of Auckland

I work on empirical asset pricing and quantitative trading. Most of my research asks what machine-learning models actually pick up in the cross-section of stock returns, and when adding more firm characteristics stops helping. I also work on market making and inventory control with limit order book data, and on portfolio choice with reinforcement learning.

Before Auckland I worked at S&P Global, Kaiyuan Securities and Volvo Group, and as a research assistant at CUHK (Shenzhen) and XJTLU.
